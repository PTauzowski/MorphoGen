Prepare a final editor-style audit report for the manuscript.

Structure:
A. Executive verdict
B. Top 5 critical issues
C. Section-by-section summary
D. Cross-paper consistency summary
E. Style/readability diagnosis
F. Reviewer-risk forecast
G. Minimal revision plan
H. Nice-to-have improvements

Do not rewrite the paper.
